Policies

Attendance

Class attendance allows students and the instructor to exchange views. Attendance is graded (1 point per in-person session). Repeated tardiness or partial attendance will reduce points. Persistent tardiness can result in up to a letter grade loss. An essay-based make-up assignment is available for up to 3 absences by emailing the instructor.

Academic Honesty & Plagiarism

Work done in this course must be your own (or your team's). You must acknowledge when you are building on someone else’s ideas. Plagiarism is passing off another's ideas or writings as your own and is not tolerated. Academic misconduct is not condoned.

Disability Accommodations

If you have a disability requiring accommodations, consult with the instructor during the 1st week of class and contact the KOKUA Program at (808) 956-7511 or visit hawaii.edu/kokua.

Grievance Policy

Students must meet directly with the instructor as the first step in resolving a grievance. If unresolved, contact the HDFS program coordinator Prof. Rheta Kuwahara to mediate.

Title IX

UHM is committed to a learning environment free of sex discrimination and gender-based violence. If you or someone you know experiences any of these, staff and resources are available on campus to support you, including confidential resources.
